## Summary of Core Principles

1. **If you have a physical model, use optimal control (LQR / MPC):** Zero training samples, certifiable stability margins, optimal energy consumption, and explicit constraint satisfaction.
2. **If the system has unmeasured states or sensor noise, use optimal filtering (Kalman / EKF / UKF):** Naive derivatives destroy actuators; Kalman filtering is the optimal bridge between reality and control.
3. **If you need non-convex global maneuvers, use energy shaping or RL:** Linearization fails far from equilibrium; energy pumping or policy optimization discovers global swing-ups.
4. **If you deploy learned models or RL, use safety shields:** Never trust an uncertified neural network near safety boundaries; supervisory shields guarantee forward invariance while retaining learned agility.
